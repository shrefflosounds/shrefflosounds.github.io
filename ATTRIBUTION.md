# Attribution & Credits

Everything borrowed, referenced, or built upon for Sidehop:

## 1. Design & Interaction Influences
- **Super Mario Maker** (Nintendo, 2015)  
  *Influence:* Seamless in-place playtesting, snap-to-grid entity placement, and retracting tool drawers during test play.
- **Kid Pix 3D / Kid Pix Studio** (Craig Hickman / Broderbund, 1989/1994)  
  *Influence:* Unapologetic tactile joy, chunky UI panels, wacky stamps, squishy audio feedback, and spontaneous creative play.
- **Drawn to Life** (5th Cell / THQ, 2007)  
  *Influence:* Player-authored hero characters and custom hand-drawn platforms living inside the physics world.
- **WarioWare D.I.Y.** (Nintendo, 2009)  
  *Influence:* Accessible micro-creation tools, simplified animation rules, and behavioral parameter tweaking.

## 2. Browser Technologies & APIs Used
- **Canvas 2D Context:** Direct rendering of platformer terrain, physics collision lines, camera transforms, and squash/stretch matrices.
- **Web Audio API:** Zero-dependency audio synthesizer generating real-time square-wave chiptune chords, frequency-swept jump boings, noise-based impact thuds, and fanfare chimes.
- **MediaRecorder API:** In-browser canvas capture and WebM video stream encoding.
- **LocalStorage:** Client-side local storage of level presets and preferences with zero remote server data transmission.
- **Service Worker & Web App Manifest:** PWA offline caching and dock/homescreen installation.
